

# Kakuma Made Creative — Multi-Page Website

## Design
- **Color palette**: Warm sunset tones — deep orange, amber, golden yellow, deep red-brown, with cream/off-white backgrounds
- **Typography**: Bold headings, clean readable body text
- **Style**: Warm, inviting, community-focused with smooth transitions and subtle animations

## Pages & Navigation

### Navigation Bar (sticky)
- Logo + "Kakuma Made Creative" text on left
- Menu items: **Home**, **About** (dropdown), **Programs** (dropdown), **Impact**, **Get Involved** (dropdown), **Donate**, **Contact**
- Dropdown menus on click (not hover)
- Mobile hamburger menu

#### Dropdown contents:
- **About**: Who We Are, Our Story, Mission & Vision, Our Values
- **Programs**: Creative Projects, Education & Learning, Environmental Initiatives, Storytelling & Media
- **Get Involved**: Volunteer, Partner With Us, Support a Youth
